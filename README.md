# CarDash beta

CarDash is a CarPlay-style dashboard for your car's web browser. Your jailbroken iPhone serves it over its own
Personal Hotspot. It was built for the Tesla browser, and any device on the hotspot can open it.

## ⬇️ [Download CarDash 1.0.1 beta (zip, 166 KB)](https://github.com/cfluid101/cardash-beta/releases/download/v1.0.1-beta/CarDash-1.0.1-beta.zip)

The zip holds the `.deb` and `README.txt`. The README covers installing, using and removing CarDash, plus the
checklist of what to report.

**Requirements:**
- A rootless jailbreak on iOS 15 or later, such as Dopamine or palera1n rootless.
- PreferenceLoader.
- Personal Hotspot.

**Quick start:**
1. Install the `.deb` with Sileo, Zebra or Filza.
2. Turn on Personal Hotspot and connect the car to it.
3. Open **`http://172.20.10.1:8765/`** in the car's browser.
4. Choose apps in **Settings → CarDash**.

> **Security:** anyone connected to your hotspot can see and control your iPhone through CarDash. Use a strong
> hotspot password.

This is a beta and not real CarPlay. Use it at your own risk.
