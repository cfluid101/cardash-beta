CarDash V3 Beta 5Connecting to iPhone…

Native CarPlay, from your iPhone.

Open CarDash V3 on the phone and tap Prepare CarPlay once. Then press Start here.

Audio stays on the iPhone or its connected Bluetooth device by default. Choose Browser audio to stream sound here; changing output reconnects CarPlay.

Microphone and Siri voice input are not supported in this beta.

Use a trusted Personal Hotspot: connected devices can control this preview. Your original CarDash installation stays separate.
